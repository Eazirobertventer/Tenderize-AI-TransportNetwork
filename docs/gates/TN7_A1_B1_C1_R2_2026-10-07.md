### TN7-C1 — Association Explorer

New operator workflow:

1. choose/search province;
2. choose municipality/city;
3. switch automatically into Association Explorer for area drill-down;
4. select association;
5. load canonical association detail;
6. highlight linked mapped ranks;
7. highlight canonical route geometries where geometry exists;
8. inspect linked rank and route lists;
9. jump from association → rank → route;
10. fit the association network to the map.

Association detail returns:

- association identity and registration;
- geographic location basis;
- linked rank count;
- linked ranks including location-pending rows;
- canonical route count;
- canonical route records and geometry when available;
- association-level source provenance.

Derived association centroids remain explicitly labelled and are never represented as office locations.

### TN7-C1 — Street Inspection v2

Mapped ranks now expose one inspection cluster:

- Google Street View;
- satellite inspection in the operational map;
- Google Maps.

Route detail now exposes:

- Street View start;
- satellite start;
- Street View end;
- satellite end;
- fit route on map.

Street/satellite imagery remains operational context only. It does not upgrade evidence status.
